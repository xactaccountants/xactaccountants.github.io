Key UK Deadlines for a Small Limited Company

Missing a filing deadline means a penalty, so keep these dates in view. Always check the exact date for your own company, because it depends on your year end.

FilingWhen it is due
Annual accounts to Companies House9 months after your company's financial year end
Confirmation statementAt least once every 12 months
Corporation Tax payment9 months and 1 day after the end of your accounting period
Corporation Tax return (CT600)12 months after the end of your accounting period
VAT returnUsually every 3 months, due 1 month and 7 days after the VAT period ends
Self-assessment tax return (online)31 January after the tax year ends
PAYE payments to HMRCMonthly, by the 22nd of the following month when paying electronically

What Do Small Business Accountants Do?

A small business accountant prepares your annual accounts, files your Corporation Tax return, keeps your records right with Companies House and HMRC, and advises on directors' pay, dividends and tax. Good accounting services also explain what the numbers mean, so you know your profit and your tax bill before the deadline arrives.

When Should You Outsource Your Bookkeeping?

It is worth outsourcing when the books take hours each week, when they are months behind, or when you do not know your profit or tax position until it is too late to act. A professional bookkeeper works on a set routine in cloud software, so the books are current and the figures are ready when you need them.
